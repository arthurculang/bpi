## Recommended first-approach trio

1. **Kevin J. Fox** (UNSW Sydney; Director, Centre for Applied Economic Research) —
   active index-number theorist (multilateral methods, scanner data, unpriced
   goods; frequent Diewert coauthor) with hands-on agency advisory experience
   (chaired Australia's CPI Review Advisory Group). Certifies the Konüs/
   conditional-index theory. Expected stance: engaged, sympathetic to new
   measurement.
2. **John S. Greenlees** (retired; former BLS Associate Commissioner for Prices
   and Living Conditions) — co-author of *"Addressing Misconceptions About the
   CPI"* (MLR 2008), the canonical rebuttal to CPI-understates-inflation claims,
   and inside knowledge of how CPI/PPI actually treated the 2008–09 bag-fee
   unbundling. **Critical prior — the referee most likely to attack the Phase 0
   benchmark before public critics do.** Thin public contact surface; reachable
   via the CRIW/NBER community.
3. **Xavier Jaravel** (LSE; Deputy President, Conseil d'analyse économique) —
   the inflation-inequality literature's central figure (QJE 2019; Annual
   Review 2021; D-CPI); validates the bottom-two-quintile weighting and
   scanner-era methods. Expected stance: sympathetic; bandwidth risk from the
   CAE role. Alternate for the critical seat if Greenlees is unreachable:
   **Daniel Sichel**.
